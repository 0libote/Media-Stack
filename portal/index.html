About this panel

Service status, library totals, and download activity are read from your connected apps. Saved keys and panel settings live in config/portal on the server. Keep that folder private and include it in your backups.

The control panel does not have access to Docker’s socket. Container restarts, VPN credentials, and disk mounts are managed on the host.
